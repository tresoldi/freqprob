# How-to guides

Task-oriented guides, each answering one practical question. They assume you
have read the [Tutorial](../getting-started/tutorial.md).

- **[Build frequency distributions](build-counts.md):** from words, n-grams,
  characters, k-mers and any iterable.
- **[Choose `bins`](choose-bins.md):** the number of possible elements, and why
  it matters.
- **[Work in log space](log-space.md):** adding log-probabilities, mixing models
  with log-sum-exp, and the floor for impossible elements.
- **[Scale to large data](scale.md):** streaming updates, vectorized and lazy
  scoring, compact storage.
- **[Save, load and refit](persist.md):** persisting fitted estimators and the
  scikit-learn-style API.
- **[Worked examples by domain](domains.md):** ecology, genomics and categorical
  data.
- **[Plot a smoothed distribution](plot.md):** observed frequencies against
  smoothed estimates.
